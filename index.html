About AlphaCare

One accountable partner for a safer, cleaner, more capable workplace.

AlphaCare Solutions (Pty) Ltd was built on a simple idea: the organisations that keep South Africa running shouldn't have to manage a different supplier for every basic need. We bring professional cleaning, private security, and digital-skills training together into one integrated, technology-enabled service — with a single point of accountability and a shared commitment to excellence.

Operating from Nonkqubela Link Mall in Khayelitsha, we are a 100% black-owned, B-BBEE Level 1 contributor serving clients across Cape Town and the Western Cape.

Our Vision

To become one of South Africa's most trusted and innovative integrated service providers within the next 10 years.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional cleaning, security, and digital-skills training that creates safer environments, healthier workplaces, and digitally empowered communities.

What We Do

Four services. One integrated standard.

Every AlphaCare service is delivered against the same standard of professionalism, reporting, and accountability — whether it's a mop, a two-way radio, or a keyboard.

Professional Cleaning

Commercial, industrial, retail and institutional cleaning built around hygiene and consistency.

  • Commercial & office cleaning
  • Industrial & post-construction cleaning
  • School & hospital hygiene programmes
  • Retail & deep cleaning
  • Waste management support

Private Security

PSIRA-registered guarding, access control and monitoring for premises of every kind.

  • Static guarding — armed & unarmed
  • Access control & visitor management
  • CCTV monitoring
  • Mobile patrols & event security
  • VIP protection & risk assessments
  • PSIRA registered personnel

Digital Skills Training

Practical computer literacy that opens doors to jobs, further study and confidence.

  • MS Office & internet/email essentials
  • Digital literacy & basic IT support
  • CV writing & job-readiness
  • Introductory AI tools
  • Cybersecurity awareness
  • SETA/QCTO-aligned where applicable

High School Tutoring

CAPS/NSC-aligned Accounting & Economics tutoring, after school and on weekends.

  • Accounting & Economics, Grades 10–12
  • CAPS/NSC-aligned curriculum
  • After-school & weekend sessions
  • 1-on-1 or small group formats
